Khet Mapper · 90-second pitch

From June to September, cloud-free satellite images of coastal Bangladesh are basically zero.

That's a real measured gap in the Sentinel-2 record — not a guess.

One cloud-proof composite, every revisit, all year.

Built on the real Copernicus pipeline — composite, analyze, report.
