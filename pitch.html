Khet Mapper · 90-second pitch

In the monsoon, coastal Bangladesh can go weeks without a single usable cloud-free satellite image.

That gap shows up in our own cloud-masked Sentinel-2 record for 2022–2024 — not a guess.

That's exactly when flood, loan, and insurance decisions get made.

Banks, insurers, and MFIs are flying blind in their highest-stakes season.

Khet Mapper uses two free Copernicus satellites: Sentinel-2 optical, which can't see through cloud, and Sentinel-1 radar, which can.

Cloudy optical pixels are dropped, radar is speckle-filtered, and each is median-composited — then stacked into one 10 m image.

It runs as three commands on Google Earth Engine: composite, analyze, report.

Smoke-tested end to end on a ~310 ha test area cut from Satkhira district — not a specific farm.

The report: area, perimeter, water surface, a year of NDVI and radar, and an exploratory flood flag.

Water is found two ways: MNDWI on clear days, radar through cloud — its threshold tuned against clear optical scenes. Not a payout call.

Insurers, MFIs, and agro-input firms already run this B2B pattern here — we license the pipeline to them, not farmers.

What's done: a working pipeline, a live demo, and an end-to-end smoke test.

What's not: the flood flag missed all 10 documented floods we tested, so it's being rebuilt. This is a pre-pilot demo.

We're looking for test cases and a retrospective pilot partner — khetmapper.tech.
